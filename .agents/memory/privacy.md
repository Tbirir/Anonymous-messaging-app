name: Anonymous inbox privacy
description: Privacy scope for Whisper and truthful messaging
---
Sender anonymity is relative to inbox recipients, not a guarantee of untraceability. Do not promise absolute anonymity.

**Why:** Anonymous messaging needs abuse prevention while keeping sender identity out of recipient-visible messages.

**How to apply:** Keep abuse-control identifiers separate from message records. Never seed fictional notes into real private inboxes; label illustrative previews clearly.
